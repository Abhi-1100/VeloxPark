# VeloxPark minimal logo

## Meaning

The mark is a compact overview of the product: the V-shaped route represents Velox movement, the open cut represents entering a parking bay, and the locator node represents a verified station in the live network.

## Use

- Use `digital/veloxpark-minimal-app-icon.svg` for the app icon, favicon tile, and avatars.
- Use `digital/veloxpark-minimal-logo.svg` for headers, landing pages, and documentation.
- Use `source/veloxpark-minimal-symbol.svg` when the symbol must stand alone.

## Palette

- Signal Gold: `#F2C230`
- Ink: `#0A0A0A`
- Paper: `#F5F5F0`

The mark is designed to work in one color as well as black on gold. Do not add shadows, gradients, outlines, or extra parking/car details.

## Minimum size

Use the app icon at 16 px or larger. Use the horizontal lockup at 120 px wide or larger. Keep clear space equal to the height of the locator node around the mark.

## Note

The SVGs are vector-only and contain no live text or raster assets. Complete a professional trademark and reverse-image search before public launch.
