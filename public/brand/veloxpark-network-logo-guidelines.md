# VeloxPark Network logo guidelines

## Meaning

The Network mark contains three parking nodes linked inside one stable frame. It represents VeloxPark’s stations, live availability data, and the connected route that gets a driver from search to a confirmed space.

## Files

- `source/veloxpark-network-symbol-color.svg` — primary color symbol.
- `source/veloxpark-network-symbol-black.svg` — black-on-light version.
- `source/veloxpark-network-symbol-white.svg` — reversed version.
- `digital/veloxpark-network-logo-horizontal.svg` — primary horizontal lockup.
- `digital/veloxpark-network-app-icon.svg` — app icon, favicon tile, and avatar.

## Palette

| Name | HEX | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Signal Gold | `#F2C230` | Brand frame/tile and primary action color |
| Ink | `#0A0A0A` | Nodes, wordmark, and dark surfaces |
| Paper | `#F5F5F0` | Reversed mark and light surfaces |

## Clear space and minimum size

Keep clear space equal to the radius of one network node on all sides. Use the symbol at 20 px or larger; use the app-icon file below 20 px. Use the horizontal lockup at 120 px wide or larger.

## Approved use

Use the color logo on Ink or Paper surfaces, the black version on Paper, and the white version on Ink. Do not separate or rearrange the three nodes.

## Avoid

Do not add extra nodes, arrows, car illustrations, gradients, shadows, outlines, rotations, or unapproved colors. Do not stretch the frame or change the spacing between nodes.

## Handover

The masters are vector-only with outlined geometry and no live text, rasters, filters, or masks. Complete a professional trademark and reverse-image search before public launch; confirm print colors with a physical proof.
