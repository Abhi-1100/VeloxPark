# VeloxPark logo guidelines

## Idea

The V-Lane mark turns the first letter of Velox into a pair of forward-moving lanes. The gold lane cuts reinforce speed, navigation, and a confident arrival without drawing a literal car or parking sign.

## Files

- `source/veloxpark-symbol-color.svg` — primary symbol artwork.
- `source/veloxpark-symbol-black.svg` — black-on-light backgrounds.
- `source/veloxpark-symbol-white.svg` — reversed use on dark backgrounds.
- `digital/veloxpark-logo-horizontal-color.svg` — horizontal logo lockup.
- `digital/veloxpark-app-icon.svg` — app icon and avatar tile.
- `digital/veloxpark-favicon.svg` — browser favicon.

## Palette

| Name | HEX | RGB | CMYK approximation | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Signal Gold | `#F2C230` | 242, 194, 48 | 0, 20, 80, 5 | Primary mark and actions |
| Ink | `#0A0A0A` | 10, 10, 10 | 0, 0, 0, 96 | Wordmark, dark surfaces |
| Paper | `#F5F5F0` | 245, 245, 240 | 0, 0, 2, 4 | Reversed mark and light surfaces |

Pantone values are intentionally not assigned until a physical print proof is available.

## Clear space and minimum size

Use clear space equal to the width of one gold lane dash around the symbol or lockup. Do not let UI borders, text, or other icons enter that zone.

| Version | Screen minimum | Print minimum |
|---|---:|---:|
| Horizontal lockup | 120 px wide | 30 mm wide |
| Symbol | 16 px, use favicon for smaller sizes | 6 mm |
| App icon | 32 px | 10 mm |

## Approved use

Use the color lockup on Ink or calm Paper surfaces. Use the black symbol on Paper. Use the white symbol on Ink. Use the app icon on Signal Gold only.

## Avoid

Do not stretch, squash, rotate, recolor outside the palette, add shadows or gradients, redraw the lane dashes, place the lockup on busy imagery, or recreate the wordmark by typing it in a different font.

## Type pairing

For product UI, retain the existing VeloxPark system: condensed display headings with a neutral sans-serif body. The logo wordmark is outlined geometry and does not depend on a font license.

## Handover notes

The SVG masters are vector-only and contain no live text, rasters, filters, or masks. A professional trademark and reverse-image search should be completed before public launch. CMYK/Pantone values should be proofed against the actual print substrate.
